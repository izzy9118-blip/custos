# Custos entry point

Read `CUSTOS.md` and `custos.yaml` before working in this repository. `CUSTOS.md`
is the active instruction document; the attachments and archived commits do not
supersede it.

The Strauss repository consumes this Reader through `custos context`, `custos
prepare`, and `custos read`. Keep the Reader independent of Strauss ministerial
reporting. See `integrations/strauss/README.md` for the integration contract.
